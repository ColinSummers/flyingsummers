# Search & Google Indexing for flyingsummers.com

## Site Search

A magnifying glass in the navbar opens a CSS-only search dialog. The form submits to Google with `sitesearch=flyingsummers.com`, so results are scoped to the site. No JS required beyond what Google provides.

## Sitemap

`sitemap.xml` is generated by `convert_batch.py` on each run. It lists every post and page with `<lastmod>` dates. After deploying, submit it to Google Search Console.

## Google Search Console Setup

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add property"
3. Choose "Domain" and enter `flyingsummers.com`
4. Google will ask you to verify via DNS TXT record
5. In DNSimple, add a TXT record:
   - Name: (leave blank, or `@` for the root)
   - Content: the string Google gives you (looks like `google-site-verification=abc123...`)
   - TTL: 3600
6. Click Verify in Search Console. May take a few minutes for DNS to propagate.
7. Once verified, go to Sitemaps in the left nav
8. Enter `https://flyingsummers.com/sitemap.xml` and click Submit
9. Google will crawl and index the site over the next few days

### What Search Console gives you
- Which pages are indexed and which have errors
- Search queries that lead to your site
- Click-through rates and average position
- Mobile usability issues
- Manual actions (spam flags, etc.)

Check back after a week to see indexing progress. You can also use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing of specific pages immediately.

## Meta Descriptions

Each post page gets a `<meta name="description">` tag using the post excerpt. This is what Google shows as the snippet in search results. The converter generates these automatically.
