Completing the Trip

August 4, 2022

If you read this entry you saw Dexter's plague doctor in various spots as he crossed the country. After a summer of research on San Juan Island, Dexter returned to the east coast (Kenmore Air & JetBlue), spent a week in New Hampshire with friends, and then needed to return to Ottawa.

Excellent weather (and a slight tailwind) for the flight. And now we will settle in for regular visits and transfers. Not needing the PCR test to cross the border is a nice change, making it possible to make a trip in the morning with a little planning the night before. I'd still like to be able to land at the little airport instead of the huge one.

(Looking over Champlain, FBO in Ottawa, and just before the return flight.)
