Tip of the Finger Lake

August 6, 2008 Flight, Trip

This is the view from our hotel room window, overlooking the Cornell campus. I haven't been back for twenty years, since I escaped.

We landed last night ahead of a thunderstorm which we chased along the length of New York State.

We took off from Champaign after lunch and landed first in Cleveland, OH. The second photo is Dexter at the rock and roll hall of fame.

Last night we walked around half of the campus and into the little college town. This morning we will stroll the main quad before climbing back into 1RD for one last leg.
