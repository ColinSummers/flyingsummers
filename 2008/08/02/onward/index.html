Onward!

August 2, 2008 Just Words

Well, if you are reading this on the web page (as opposed to in a newsreader like Google's Reader), then you've noticed a layout change. I upgraded the software (WordPress) which publishes the blog. Oh, exciting, I know. But, it does mean that I can write entries from the iPhone.

Who wouldn't be close enough to a computer to blog? Me! Actually, us! The entire family is going to climb into the plane tomorrow and fly from Santa Monica, California to Newport, New Hampshire (spitting distance to Pleasant Lake, where Nell's family has a little house on a lake).

We have no idea how it will work out, so it is a real adventure. Our planned route:


  • Santa Monica, CA

  • Sedona, AZ

  • Albuquerque, NM

  • Witchita, KS

  • Chicago, IL

  • Ohio (I know, not very specific)

  • Ithaca, NY

  • New Hampshire!


Tomorrow morning our departure is 8:00am from KSMO. We hope to touch down in Sedona for a brunch snack at 10:30am. That's where you'll see an entry from the iPhone. Maybe. We'll see. It's entirely possible that in Albuquerque the boys will rebel and demand a JetBlue flight direct to Boston and some quality time with Nell in Cambridge while I continue across solo. Only time will tell.
